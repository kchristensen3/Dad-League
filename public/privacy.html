Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 25, 2026 • Effective Date: September 25, 2026

Welcome to Dad League ("we," "our," or "us"). Dad League is a community platform designed to help dads take time for themselves, connect with friends, and coordinate weekly activities and meetups.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about the data we collect, why we collect it, and how you control it. This Privacy Policy applies to the Dad League progressive web application, iOS application, and related services (collectively, the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide, maintain, and improve the Dad League community experience:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google Authentication, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo URL.
  • Profile Information: You may optionally provide your local area/huddle location (e.g., city/neighborhood), weekly availability preferences, and personal interests/hobbies (e.g., golf, trivia, board games, BBQ).
  • Community Content: Information you actively post within your league, including proposed activity ideas, event RSVP responses, messages in group chat threads, comments on community boards, and photos/links you share.

B. Automatically Collected Information

  • App Usage & Device Data: Basic technical data such as browser type, operating system, and timestamp logs necessary to ensure service reliability, maintain security, and optimize performance.
  • Push Notification Tokens: If you explicitly opt in to receive event reminders or meetup notifications, we store a notification registration token. You can disable notifications at any time in your device or browser settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for the following purposes:

  • Facilitating League Connections: Displaying member rosters, organizing weekly activities, and managing RSVP headcounts for events.
  • Communication: Delivering messages, group chats, community board updates, and optional meetup notifications.
  • Account Management: Authenticating your identity and maintaining your settings and preferences.
  • Security & Integrity: Preventing abuse, spam, fraud, or violations of our Terms of Service.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data to data brokers or third parties. We do not use your information for third-party advertising or profiling.

3. Service Providers & Third-Party Integrations

We share information with trusted third-party infrastructure providers solely to operate our service:

  • Google Cloud & Firebase: We use Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (hosted by Google Cloud) to securely manage user authentication and store application data with encrypted transit and storage.
  • Google Maps Platform: If enabled for location search and meetup spot discovery, address queries may be processed via Google Maps Platform in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your data only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with the services.

In-App Account & Data Deletion (Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v))

You have the absolute right to delete your account at any time. Inside the App, navigate to Account Settings (Profile) → Account & Data Privacy and select Delete Account. This permanently removes your member record, interests, availability, and Google sign-in credentials from our database.

You can also submit a deletion request via email to kylechristensen3@gmail.com.

5. Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal data. Authentication is handled via secure OAuth providers (Google Sign-In), and all communication with our servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

6. Children's Privacy

Dad League is intended for adults (parents and guardians aged 18 and older). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child, we will take immediate steps to delete it.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to access, update, export, or delete your personal information, or withdraw consent at any time without discrimination.

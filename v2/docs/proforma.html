Income statement

Annual totals. Amounts in US dollars.

How it works: each month, new leagues sign up (growing at the signup growth rate), some free leagues go quiet, a share of free leagues upgrade to League Pro, and some Pro leagues cancel. Annual plans are counted at 1/12 per month. App Store fees apply to the iOS share of new paid leagues once the iOS app launches. Donations go straight to partner nonprofits through a donation platform, so they're shown as impact, not income, unless you set a donor tip to Dad League. This is a planning model, not accounting or tax advice.

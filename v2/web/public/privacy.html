Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026 · Effective November 24, 2026

Dad League ("we," "us") is a small app that helps groups of dads plan meetups, share ideas and keep in touch. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. It covers the Dad League website and app at dad-league-production.up.railway.app and any mobile version of it (together, "Dad League").

The short version: we collect what's needed to run your league and nothing more. Your league members see what you post in that league. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use advertising trackers. You can delete your account and what you've posted from your Profile at any time.

1. Information we collect

You give us

  • Account details when you sign in with Google or Apple: your name, email address and profile photo. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email," we receive a relay address instead of your real one, and Apple only shares your name the first time you sign in.
  • Profile details you choose to add: where you live (for example "Ferndale, MI"), when you're usually free, and your interests.
  • What you create in a league: events and RSVPs, standing meetups, ideas and votes, board posts, likes and comments, chat messages, photos, and the places you pick for events.

Collected as you use Dad League

  • Photos. When you upload a photo, we resize it and remove the hidden information phones attach to pictures, including the location where it was taken.
  • Notification registration. If you turn on notifications, your browser gives us a delivery address for your device. We store it to send you notifications and delete it if it stops working or you delete your account.
  • Technical records. Our servers record basic request information (such as the page requested, the time and whether it succeeded) to keep the service running and secure. Our hosting provider may also record IP addresses in its network logs.
  • Cookies and similar storage. We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in (it lasts up to 30 days and renews while you use the app) and short-lived cookies during sign-in. Your browser also remembers which league you last opened and whether you've seen the welcome page. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

2. How we use it

  • To run your leagues: show events, ideas, posts, chat, photos, member lists, stats and the Trophy Case to the members of each league.
  • To send notifications you've turned on, such as new events, day-before reminders, host turns and chat messages. You can switch each type off in your Profile.
  • To provide optional AI features (see section 4) and place search (see section 5).
  • To keep Dad League secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To communicate with you about your account or important changes to Dad League.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

3. Who can see what

  • Members of your league can see your name, profile photo, the profile details you added, your activity stats, and everything you post in that league. If you're in several leagues, each league only sees what's posted in it.
  • Anyone with your league's invite link can see the league's name, description and member count before joining.
  • League owners can remove members and remove content in their league.

4. AI features (Anthropic)

Dad League uses Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, for two optional features. Anthropic processes this information as our service provider to produce a response for us.

  • Idea suggestions (the "Suggest" button on the Ideas tab). We send the league's name and description, members' stated locations, availability and interests, and the titles of ideas already on the list. We don't send names, email addresses or chat messages.
  • The Commissioner (a weekly note in league chat, which league owners can turn off). We send the league's name and time zone, members' names and stated availability, upcoming events with who's in, who's out and who hasn't replied, the top-voted ideas, the number of chat messages that week, and the Commissioner's previous note. We don't send email addresses, chat message text or photos.

AI-written suggestions and notes can be wrong. Check details such as venue hours before you go.

5. Other service providers

We share information only with providers that help us run Dad League, and only what they need:

  • Railway hosts our servers, database and photo storage in the United States.
  • Google provides Sign in with Google. If place search is on, the text you type into a location field and the place you pick are sent from our server to Google Maps Platform to find places and draw map previews. Google receives these requests from our server, not your device.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple.
  • Push notification services run by your browser or device maker (Apple, Google or Mozilla) deliver notifications. The contents are encrypted so these services can't read them.
  • Links you post. When you post a link, our server visits that page to show its title and image as a preview.
  • Payments. Dad League is free today. If we add paid features or donations, payments will be handled by a payment processor (such as Stripe or Apple) and we will not store your full card number. We'll update this policy before that happens.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a merger or sale of Dad League (in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information).

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete something, it's removed from Dad League right away; copies in backups and logs may remain for a limited time before they are overwritten.

7. Deleting your account

Go to Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, profile, notification registrations, and the ideas, events you host, posts, comments, chat messages and photos you've added. If you own a league, it passes to the member who has been in it longest; if you're the only member, the league is deleted. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

8. Security

Connections to Dad League are encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in is handled by Google or Apple, so we never see or store a password. Photos and notification addresses are only served to members of the right league. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't post anything you wouldn't want your league to see.

9. Age requirement

Dad League is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, or the EU and UK), you may have the right to know what we hold about you, get a copy, correct it, delete it, or object to how it's used. You can edit or delete most information yourself in the app. For anything else, email us and we'll respond within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for using these rights. We don't "sell" or "share" personal information as those terms are used in California law.

11. International users

Dad League is operated from, and stores information in, the United States. If you use it from elsewhere, your information will be processed in the United States.

12. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app before they take effect.
